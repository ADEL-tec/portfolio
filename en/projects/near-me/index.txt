The map draws two concentric circles on the customer's position: an outer discovery circle, adjustable from 1 km to 100 km, that pins every interest-matched company nearby, and an inner alert circle, tunable from 1 m to 500 m, that fires a sounded notification the moment a matching business is crossed. Alerts keep working when the app is terminated — the ENTER events come from the OS geofencing APIs through a background callback dispatcher, not from a foreground poll. Companies get a dashboard with impression analytics and top-performing branches, multi-branch management with map-pinned locations, and a profile they maintain themselves. A companion Flutter admin panel closes the loop: company and branch registrations land in an append-only activities feed where an administrator approves them before they go live.
