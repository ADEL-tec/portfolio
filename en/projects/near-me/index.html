Near Me — Proximity Discovery & Geofenced Alerts

Dual-radius geofencing that alerts shoppers when a matching business is nearby

  • Published
  • Background Geofencing
  • Dual-Role Architecture
  • Clean Architecture + BLoC
  • Bilingual with RTL

Near Me connects shoppers with the businesses physically around them. The map draws two concentric circles on the customer's position: an outer discovery circle, adjustable from 1 km to 100 km, that pins every interest-matched company nearby, and an inner alert circle, tunable from 1 m to 500 m, that fires a sounded notification the moment a matching business is crossed. Alerts keep working when the app is terminated — the ENTER events come from the OS geofencing APIs through a background callback dispatcher, not from a foreground poll. The app ships two roles from one codebase. Customers run a quick-interests onboarding flow, browse stores and branch details, and manage a live notification feed. Companies get a dashboard with impression analytics and top-performing branches, multi-branch management with map-pinned locations, and a profile they maintain themselves. A companion Flutter admin panel closes the loop: company and branch registrations land in an append-only activities feed where an administrator approves them before they go live. Both apps are built on Flutter and Firebase (Auth, Firestore, Storage, Cloud Messaging) and follow strict clean architecture — data, domain and presentation layers per feature, BLoC/Cubit for state, get_it for injection, and dartz Either for error handling — across roughly 50,000 lines of Dart. The interface is fully localized in English and Arabic with RTL support.
